# ShopDesk refunds policy

- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days.
- Support agents never promise a refund amount; only the refunds team decides.
- For a damaged part of an item (for example a cracked lid), offer a replacement part first; a partial refund is the alternative.
- Refunds above 150 EUR always need a human approver.
- A lost parcel is refunded in full once the carrier confirms the loss.
