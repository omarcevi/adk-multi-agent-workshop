# ShopDesk returns policy

- Customers can return any item within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused and in the original packaging, unless they arrived damaged.
- Damaged items do not need to be returned; a photo of the damage is enough.
- Exchanges for a different colour or model are free when the new item is in stock.
- Backordered items can be cancelled at any time before they ship, with a full refund.
